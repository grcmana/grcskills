GRCMANA is an AI-native governance, risk and compliance practice founded by Harry West. It maintains GRC Skills and GRC OS as free, open-source projects so that practitioners and accidental compliance owners can put AI agents to work on real GRC programmes, with a human accountable for every decision.

The core skills were built by a former Global CISO: GRCP certified, and Certified Lead Implementer for ISO 27001, ISO 27701 and ISO 42001.

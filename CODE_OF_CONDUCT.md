# Code of conduct

GRC Skills follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and remember that many contributors are new to GRC or to open source.

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/grcmana/grcskills/security/advisories/new), marked "Code of conduct". Reports are handled confidentially.

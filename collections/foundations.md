title: Foundations
order: 1
---
Every GRC programme starts with the same question: what does this organisation actually do, and what is it being asked to prove? The Foundations skills answer that question once, write the answer down, and keep it current, so every other skill works from your real context instead of a generic company.

Start with `company-context`. It builds a short company profile covering what you sell, the data you hold, your technology stack, your people, and who is asking for assurance. It then recommends a proportionate scope and set of frameworks. Every other GRC skill reads that profile silently, which is why their output talks about your systems and your customers rather than a template.

At the other end of the programme, `grc-status` rolls the whole workspace up into a one-page update. It covers progress per framework, the top risks, overdue actions and the decisions leadership needs to make. The same skill can prepare inputs for an ISO 27001 management review.

If you are new to compliance, install these two first. They take minutes to run, and they make everything else faster and more accurate.

---
Auditors don't reward long policies. They reward policies that match how the organisation really works, that name owners, and that make statements somebody can test. The Policy management skills are built around that idea, whether you are writing your first policy set or inheriting one somebody else wrote years ago.

`policy-writer` recommends the minimum viable policy set for the frameworks you have chosen, then drafts each policy for your actual stack, team size and risk profile. Drafts use plain language, assign responsibilities by role, and avoid promises the business cannot keep.

`policy-gap-review` reads the policies you already have the way an auditor would. It checks coverage against your frameworks, flags statements that cannot be evidenced, spots contradictions between documents, and produces a prioritised list of fixes.

Every draft is a starting point for review by a competent person. The skills never mark a policy as approved; that decision, and the approval fields that record it, belong to your leadership.

Some obligations come with their own specific workflow. The Frameworks skills handle those framework-specific jobs in depth, building on the shared company context and risk method used across GRC Skills.

`cyber-essentials-scoping` helps UK organisations define the scope of a Cyber Essentials assessment and find the issues that cause automatic failures, such as unsupported software, cloud services without multi-factor authentication, or critical updates not installed within 14 days, before you submit. It works from the current requirements version and question set, which change every April.

`dpia` screens new projects to decide whether a GDPR data protection impact assessment is required, then drafts one for your data protection officer or privacy lead to review. It covers the UK and EU regimes and keeps the focus on risk to individuals rather than risk to the company.

Each skill carries an own-words framework pack with an `as_of` date, so you can see when the knowledge was last checked against the official source. Neither skill gives legal advice or certifies anything; they prepare well-structured drafts for the people accountable for those decisions.

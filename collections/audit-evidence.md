---
title: Audit & evidence
order: 4
---
Audits are won or lost on evidence. The Audit & evidence skills help you understand how ready you are, plan what to collect, and check what you have collected before an auditor sees it, so fieldwork is about confirming good practice rather than chasing screenshots.

`audit-readiness` scores your readiness for a chosen framework, explains the gaps in plain terms, and lays out a realistic roadmap to close them. It is useful months before an audit and again a few weeks out.

`evidence-request-list` turns your framework selection into a deduplicated, control-mapped list of evidence and a tracker. Each item has an owner, a source system, a frequency and a due date, so one artefact can serve several frameworks at once.

`evidence-review` checks collected evidence against what auditors typically expect: right period, right population, complete, legible and attributable. It flags weak items early, while there is still time to fix them.

None of these skills can tell you that you will pass. They help you walk into the audit organised, honest about gaps, and able to explain how every artefact was produced.

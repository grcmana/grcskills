Risk management is the engine of ISO 27001, ISO 42001 and a credible SOC 2 programme. It is also where most early-stage programmes go wrong: either a spreadsheet of generic threats nobody owns, or nothing at all. The Risk management skills give you a method an auditor will recognise and a register your team will actually use.

`risk-register` identifies risks from your company context, scores them with a consistent likelihood and impact method, and proposes treatments that map to the controls in each framework you have chosen. Owners and acceptance decisions stay with people, and the skill leaves approval fields blank for them to complete.

`ai-impact-assessment` does the same job for artificial intelligence. It inventories the AI systems you build and buy, then assesses their risks and their impact on the people affected by them, in line with ISO 42001 and with GDPR where personal data is involved.

Both skills write plain CSV and Markdown into your private `grc/` folder, so the register stays yours, versionable and easy to hand to an auditor.
